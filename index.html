About

I'm Rovin Singh, from Chandauli, Uttar Pradesh. I studied at Mahatma Gandhi Kashi Vidyapith, Varanasi, and started writing news and online content in 2017.

After years of preparing for SSC, Railway and State Police exams, I cleared SSC GD 2018 and joined the Border Security Force in 2022. I served for a year and left in 2023 for personal reasons.

Leaving the uniform meant starting over. I went back to writing, and in 2024 I co-founded DIGIVILL TECH with Ritesh Yadav and Rahul Yadav. There I look after growth and finance: helping our products reach more people and keeping the company's numbers in order.

This blog is the personal side of that story: what worked, what didn't, and what I'd tell someone who is starting today.
