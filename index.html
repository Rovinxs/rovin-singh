About

I'm Rovin Singh, from Chandauli, Uttar Pradesh. I started writing news and online content in 2017.

After years of preparing for SSC, Railway and State Police exams, I cleared SSC GD 2018 and joined the Border Security Force in 2022. I served for a year and left in 2023 for personal reasons.

In 2024 I co-founded DIGIVILL TECH LLP. After that I built SarkariJob.com, followed by MyRTOBuddy.com and DigiIDNow.com.
