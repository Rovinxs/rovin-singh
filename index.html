About

From aspirant to entrepreneur

I'm Rovin Singh, from Chandauli, Uttar Pradesh. My journey with competitive exams and digital content began in 2017, when I started writing about news and online topics. What began as curiosity soon became a mission to help fellow aspirants.

After years of preparing for SSC, Railway and State Police exams, I cleared SSC GD 2018 and joined the Border Security Force. I served for a year before leaving for personal reasons — an experience that taught me discipline, consistency and mental resilience.

I went on to build SarkariJob.com, MyRTOBuddy.com and DigiIDNow.com, and in 2024 I co-founded DigiVill Tech LLP, where I currently serve as Director. You can read my published work on my author page.
